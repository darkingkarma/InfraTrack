Simple Reporting Process

How InfraTrack Works

Reporting infrastructure issues is simple. Follow these steps using the InfraTrack mobile application.

1

Download and Register

Download the InfraTrack mobile application, create your citizen account, and verify your email address.

2

Submit an Infrastructure Report

Describe the infrastructure problem, attach photos, and provide the location of the affected road, bridge, or drainage system.

3

Report Screening and Inspection

The Frontline Receiving Unit screens submitted reports, while Field Engineers inspect validated issues.

4

Track Report Progress

Follow the status of your report and receive updates as the reported issue moves through the repair process.

About InfraTrack

Connecting Citizens and Infrastructure Monitoring

InfraTrack is a GIS-Enabled Infrastructure Issue Reporting and Monitoring System developed for the DPWH Iligan City District Office.

The system aims to provide a centralized platform where citizens can submit infrastructure concerns and authorized personnel can screen, inspect, manage, and monitor reported issues.

Through location-based reporting, photo documentation, and report status monitoring, InfraTrack supports a more organized process for handling infrastructure concerns across Iligan City.
